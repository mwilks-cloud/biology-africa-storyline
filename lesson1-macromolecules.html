1. Carbon Atoms & Usable Energy Rule

Rule from Background Reading: Chemical bonds store energy. When bonds break during cellular respiration, energy is released. The more Carbon atoms in a molecule, the more potential energy it provides!

Monosaccharide (Glucose - Carbohydrate)

C: 6 H: 12 O: 6

Relative Energy Yield: Moderate (~4 kcal/g)

Fatty Acid / Monoglyceride (Lipid)

C: 16 H: 32 O: 2

Relative Energy Yield: Very High (~9 kcal/g due to dense C-H chain!)

Amino Acid (Glycine - Protein)

C: 2 H: 5 O: 2 N: 1

Relative Energy Yield: Primary use is structural body building, not fast energy storage.

2. Digestion Reaction: Breaking Plant Sucrose

When an elephant eats plant matter, enzymes break down complex disaccharides like Sucrose through hydrolysis reactions into simple sugar monomers.

Chemical Reaction:

Sucrose ($C_{12}H_{22}O_{11}$) + $H_2O$ ➔ Glucose ($C_6H_{12}O_6$) + Fructose ($C_6H_{12}O_6$)
